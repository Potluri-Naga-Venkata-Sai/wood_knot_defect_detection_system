Wood Surface Defect Reference Library

Standardized taxonomy & quality sorting criteria recognized by the model

Sound Knot

Living Branch Junction

An intergrown knot formed by living branches, firmly cemented into the timber body. Retains strong fiber continuity.

Industry Grade: Acceptable for structural framing
Dead Knot

Encased / Loose Knot

Formed from a dead limb; ringed with black bark or voids. Prone to loosening, drying fallout, and high stress notches.

Industry Grade: Requires resin stabilization or trim
Crack / Split

Separation of Fibers

Longitudinal rupture along radial wood rays from uneven moisture release or tension during kiln seasoning.

Industry Grade: Reject for structural load-bearing
Pin Hole

Insect Boreholes (< 2mm)

Fine perforations bored by ambrosia beetles in unseasoned green wood. Minor structural defect unless infestation is active.

Industry Grade: Verify insect inactivity; kiln heat-treat
Rot / Decay

Fungal Decomposition

Enzymatic breakdown of timber lignin and cellulose. Results in spongy or brittle cubical fracture and total strength loss.

Industry Grade: Immediate Cull / Industrial Discard
